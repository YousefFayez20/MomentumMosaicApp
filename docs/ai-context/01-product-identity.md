# Product Identity

Momentum Mosaic is a **Daily Discipline System**.

It is not a generic task manager, passive analytics dashboard, habit toy, life operating system, second brain, or productivity content platform. It exists to help an individual decide what matters today, enter focused execution, protect their attention, and build sustained momentum through consistent follow-through.

---

## 1. Product Positioning

Momentum Mosaic sits at the intersection of a daily planner, an active focus timer, a physical discipline tracker, and an algorithmic momentum gauge. Its value is not simply recording what happened in the past. Its value is guiding the user through the core daily loop:

```text
Plan  -->  Commit  -->  Execute  -->  Complete  -->  Reflect
```

The system acts like a serious, high-standard training partner: calm, direct, structured, and quietly encouraging.

---

## 2. Core Philosophy

- **Today over lifetime stats**: The primary surface must answer: *"What does today require?"* Lifetime numbers are secondary reference data.
- **Execution over curation**: The system must help the user act, not spend hours organizing, tagging, or gardening notes.
- **One active commitment**: A focus session protects the current task. The user is either executing or planning—never split between competing priorities.
- **Momentum through consistency**: Meaningful progress is built from repeated daily signals, not occasional bursts of heroics.
- **Mathematical dignity**: Momentum is calculated through algorithmic consistency across multiple behavioral signals (focus, tasks, workouts), not childish streaks or flashy gamification.
- **Dignified feedback**: Acknowledgement is precise, calm, and earned.
- **Simplicity as discipline**: If a feature adds friction or maintenance burden without directly strengthening daily execution, it does not belong.

---

## 3. Emotional Direction

### What the product feels like:
- **Grounded & Calm**: Dark, quiet surfaces, restrained accents, and minimal cognitive noise.
- **Focused & Purposeful**: Clear visual hierarchy where the next action is always obvious.
- **Premium but Restrained**: High craftsmanship in typography, subtle glows, and deliberate spacing.
- **Serious without being cold**: High expectations, but deeply respectful of the user's focus and energy.
- **Supportive without cheerleading**: Acknowledges real effort without empty slogans.

### What the product rejects:
- Noisy gamification (confetti, badges, arbitrary XP points, leveling up).
- Childish or overly playful micro-copy.
- Corporate or bureaucratic task management clutter (Kanban boards with 20 columns, epic hierarchies).
- Notion-style infinite nesting, block databases, or graph views that invite procrastination.
- Social comparison, leaderboards, or public performance pressure.

---

## 4. Behavioral Goals

Momentum Mosaic empowers the user to:
1. **See the day clearly**: Review planned tasks and daily workout status upon opening the application.
2. **Choose the next commitment**: Select a task and enter an active focus session.
3. **Protect attention during execution**: Use distraction-free workspace views (Deep Writing mode, auto-hiding navigation, and synchronized session timers).
4. **Complete work with closure**: Finish focus blocks with clean closure and logged actual focus duration.
5. **Notice momentum compounding**: Watch the algorithmic momentum score respond to consistent execution over rolling windows.
6. **Recover without shame**: Rebuild momentum smoothly after missed days without penalizing shame mechanics.

---

## 5. Anti-Goals

The system explicitly does **NOT** optimize for:
- Unlimited backlog capture or inbox hoarding.
- Multi-level nested folder hierarchies.
- Knowledge graph construction or bi-directional linking.
- Complex rich-text formatting or media scrapbooking.
- Social sharing, follower feeds, or team delegation workflows.
- Passive analytics dashboards that show data without driving immediate action.

---

## 6. Product Voice & Tone

The voice is that of an elite personal coach: calm, articulate, grounded, and concise.

| Preferred Voice | Prohibited Voice |
|---|---|
| "Deep focus session complete. 45m logged." | "Woohoo! You're a rockstar! 🎉" |
| "Momentum steady. 2 tasks remaining today." | "Don't give up! You got this champ!" |
| "Workout logged. Streak protected." | "AMAZING job crushing those weights! 💪" |
| "Ready when you are." | "Let's smash our goals today!" |

---

## 7. Strategic Boundary & Feature Filter

Before introducing any new capability or view, test it against this filter:
1. *Does this help the user know what to do today?*
2. *Does this help the user protect focus right now?*
3. *Does this provide honest feedback on consistency?*

If a feature fails all three questions, it does not belong in the core product.
